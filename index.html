MATHEMATICAL SIGNAL CONDITIONING

ALGORITHMS
1. Voltage Divider & Hydrogen PPM Calculation Vout = (ADC * 3.3 / 4095) * 1.5 PPM_H2 = 900.0 * (Rs / R0)^(-2.1)

Compensates 1kΩ / 2kΩ divider resistance and non-linear semiconductor curve.

2. 1D Discrete Kalman Filter (Drift Suppression) Q = 0.1 (Process Noise) | R = 20.0 (Measurement Noise)

Eliminates thermal jitter and drift while preserving true gas transients.

3. Multi-Modal Risk Scoring Formula Risk = (0.40 * GasRisk) + (0.30 * SwellRisk) + (0.20 * TrendRisk) + Boost

+20% Correlation Boost added when Gas > 20% and Swell > 20% simultaneously.

COMPLIANCE, TERMS OF SERVICE & PRIVACY POLICY

Operational safety boundaries, data governance, and liability disclosures

TERMS OF SERVICE (TOS)

SAFETY ADVISORY

1. Operational Intended Use: The Multi-Modal Chemical-Physical Correlation Engine is an edge-based industrial telemetry and early hazard advisory system. It is designed to assist battery management systems (BMS) and operators in early detection of cell venting and swelling.

2. Hardware Relays & Secondary Interlocks: Hardware safety interlocks (such as the GPIO 21 exhaust fan relay) operate deterministically. Machine learning inference outputs (Isolation Forest anomaly confidence) are strictly advisory and do not override hardcoded safety trip points.

3. Limitation of Liability: Sensor outputs are subject to environmental drift and clean-air reference recalibration. This software does not substitute for certified physical fire suppression infrastructure or certified safety containment protocols.

PRIVACY & TELEMETRY POLICY

DATA GOVERNANCE

1. Local Edge Processing: All sensor conditioning (Kalman filtering, median filtering, rate calculations, and machine learning inference) is performed locally on the edge node. No battery telemetry data is transmitted to external cloud services without explicit configuration.

2. Diagnostic Audit Logging: System audit logs and event ledgers are stored strictly in local memory and are accessible only by authorized local operators. No persistent user tracking cookies or third-party analytics are embedded.

3. Compliance & Audits: Telemetry logs exported by operators are formatted in standard plain text for regulatory compliance verification and post-incident investigation.
